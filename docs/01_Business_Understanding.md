# 1. Business Understanding

## 1. Business Background

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects customers with
thousands of independent sellers across multiple product categories. As
the platform has expanded, business data is distributed across multiple
operational systems, making it difficult for leadership to monitor
performance through a single, unified view.

Business functions such as sales, customer behavior, logistics, seller
operations, customer satisfaction, and payment activity are captured in
separate datasets. Without centralized reporting, identifying trends,
monitoring operational performance, and supporting strategic
decision-making becomes increasingly challenging.

To address these challenges, the analytics team has been tasked with
developing an executive-ready Business Intelligence solution that
consolidates key business metrics into a centralized dashboard.

## 2. Business Problem

Despite continued business growth, Olist lacks a centralized analytics
solution that provides a comprehensive view of business performance.

As a result, management faces challenges in:

-   Monitoring revenue and order trends.
-   Understanding customer purchasing behavior.
-   Evaluating seller performance.
-   Measuring logistics efficiency.
-   Tracking customer satisfaction.
-   Analyzing payment behavior.

Without integrated reporting, business decisions are made using
fragmented information rather than a unified view of organizational
performance.

## 3. Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to develop an end-to-end
analytics solution that provides stakeholders with a centralized view of
business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

Specifically, the project aims to:

-   Consolidate multiple business datasets into a unified analytical
    model.
-   Measure key business performance indicators.
-   Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
-   Evaluate operational performance.
-   Assess customer satisfaction.
-   Generate actionable business insights.
-   Support strategic decision-making through data visualization.

## 4. Stakeholders

This analytics solution is intended for the following stakeholders:

-   **Business Sponsor (Client):** Requests the analytics solution and
    uses it to support strategic business decisions.
-   **Data Analyst:** Responsible for data preparation, analysis,
    visualization, and reporting.
-   **Business Managers and Decision Makers:** Use the dashboard to
    monitor business performance and identify improvement opportunities.

## 5. Business Questions

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

-   How has business revenue changed over time?
-   Which products and categories contribute most to overall revenue?
-   Which customer segments contribute the most to revenue and long-term
    business value?
-   How effectively is the business retaining customers?
-   Which geographic regions offer the greatest opportunities for
    business growth?
-   How efficient is the order fulfillment process?
-   Which operational factors contribute to delayed deliveries?
-   Which sellers consistently deliver strong sales and operational
    performance?

## 6. Project Scope

This project focuses on analyzing historical e-commerce data to evaluate
sales performance, customer behavior, logistics efficiency, and customer
satisfaction. The project includes data preparation, SQL analysis,
exploratory data analysis, KPI development, and the creation of an
executive Power BI dashboard that supports business decision-making.
Predictive modeling and production deployment are outside the scope of
this project.

## 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it delivers a reliable
analytical solution that enables stakeholders to monitor business
performance through meaningful KPIs, interactive dashboards, and
actionable insights. The final solution should support informed
decision-making, demonstrate sound analytical practices, and serve as a
professional portfolio project suitable for GitHub and technical
interviews.

## 8. Data Considerations

This analysis is based on historical transaction data provided in the
Olist dataset. Any missing values, inconsistent records, or data quality
issues identified during the project will be documented and addressed as
part of the data cleaning process. The findings and recommendations are
therefore limited to the information available within the dataset.

## 9. Business Value

This project provides a centralized analytics solution that helps
stakeholders understand business performance across sales, customers,
and operations. By combining data from multiple business functions into
a single dashboard, it enables faster reporting, supports data-driven
decision-making, and helps identify opportunities to improve overall
business performance.

## 10. Future Scope

This project focuses on descriptive analytics using historical data.
Future enhancements may include predictive sales forecasting, customer
churn analysis, inventory optimization, automated data pipelines,
cloud-based deployment, and advanced statistical modeling to further
support business decision-making.

## 11. Conclusion

This document establishes the business foundation for the Olist
E-commerce Analytics project by defining the business context,
objectives, scope, stakeholders, and key analytical questions. The next
stage focuses on understanding the dataset and designing an analytical
model capable of answering these business questions.
