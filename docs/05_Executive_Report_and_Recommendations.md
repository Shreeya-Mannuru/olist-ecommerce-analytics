# 5. Executive Report and Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis evaluates the performance of an e-commerce marketplace
using sales, customer, product, operational and customer-experience
data.

The marketplace generated approximately **R\$13.22 million in
delivered-order revenue** across approximately **99K orders**, with an
average order value of approximately **R\$137.04**.

Average delivery time was approximately **12.09 days**, while the
average customer review score was **4.09/5**.

The results indicate that the marketplace has strong transaction
activity and generally positive customer feedback, while delivery
performance, customer retention and category-level performance remain
important areas for continued monitoring.

## Key Findings

### 1. Strong marketplace activity

The marketplace processed approximately 99K orders and generated
approximately R\$13.22M in delivered-order revenue.

### 2. Customer base is substantial

Approximately 96K unique customers were identified, highlighting a broad
marketplace customer base.

### 3. Customer experience is generally positive

The average review score of 4.09/5 indicates generally favorable
customer feedback.

### 4. Delivery performance remains important

An average delivery time of 12.09 days provides a useful operational
benchmark. Delivery delays should be analyzed alongside low review
scores to identify potential customer-experience issues.

### 5. Product performance is concentrated

A relatively small group of product categories contributes a significant
proportion of marketplace revenue. These categories should receive
particular attention from commercial and operational teams.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Monitor delivery performance by seller and region

Identify sellers and geographic areas with consistently longer delivery
times.

**Why:**\
Improving fulfillment performance can potentially improve customer
experience and reduce negative reviews.

### 2. Investigate low-rated orders

Analyze orders with review scores of 1--2 alongside:

-   Delivery time
-   Seller
-   Product category
-   Freight value
-   Geographic location

**Why:**\
This can help determine whether poor customer experiences are primarily
associated with delivery, seller performance or product categories.

### 3. Prioritize high-performing product categories

Monitor inventory and seller availability for the categories generating
the most revenue.

**Why:**\
Stock availability and fulfillment problems in high-demand categories
could have a larger impact on marketplace revenue.

### 4. Improve repeat-purchase analysis

Track repeat-customer behavior over time and segment customers based on
order frequency and value.

**Why:**\
Acquiring new customers is not the only way to grow marketplace revenue;
encouraging existing customers to return can improve long-term customer
value.

### 5. Evaluate payment methods using economics, not only usage

Payment methods should be assessed using transaction costs, conversion
performance, installment behavior and customer preferences before making
promotional decisions.

**Why:**\
The most frequently used payment method is not necessarily the most
profitable payment method.

## Expected Business Impact

If implemented effectively, these actions could help the marketplace:

-   Improve delivery reliability
-   Reduce poor customer experiences
-   Protect revenue from high-performing categories
-   Improve customer retention
-   Support more informed payment and seller decisions

The dataset does not contain enough information to quantify the
financial impact of these recommendations.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides a practical framework for monitoring marketplace
performance across sales, customer behavior, product categories,
delivery operations and customer satisfaction.

The recommended focus is to move beyond measuring revenue alone and
continuously evaluate how operational performance and customer
experience influence long-term marketplace performance.
