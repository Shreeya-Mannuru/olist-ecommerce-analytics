# 3. KPI Definition

## 1. KPI Framework

The KPIs were selected based on the project's business questions and
grouped into five areas:

1.  Sales Performance
2.  Customer Performance
3.  Product Performance
4.  Operational Performance
5.  Customer Experience & Payment Behavior

## 2. Sales KPIs

### Total Revenue

**Definition:**\
Total item revenue generated from delivered orders.

**Calculation:**\
Sum of item price for delivered orders.

**Business Purpose:**\
Measures the overall sales value generated by completed orders.

### Total Orders

**Definition:**\
Number of unique orders.

**Calculation:**\
Distinct count of order IDs.

**Business Purpose:**\
Measures marketplace order volume.

### Average Order Value

**Definition:**\
Average revenue generated per delivered order.

**Calculation:**\
Delivered Revenue / Delivered Orders

**Business Purpose:**\
Helps understand the average value of each completed transaction.

### Average Basket Size

**Definition:**\
Average number of order items associated with a delivered order.

**Calculation:**\
Delivered Order Items / Delivered Orders

**Business Purpose:**\
Helps understand whether customers typically purchase one product or
multiple products per order.

## 3. Customer KPIs

### Total Customers

**Definition:**\
Number of unique customers.

**Business Purpose:**\
Measures the size of the customer base.

### Repeat Customer Rate

**Definition:**\
Percentage of customers with more than one delivered order.

**Calculation:**\
Repeat Customers / Total Customers × 100

**Business Purpose:**\
Provides an indication of customer retention and repeat purchasing
behavior.

## 4. Product KPI

### Revenue by Product Category

**Definition:**\
Revenue generated by each product category.

**Business Purpose:**\
Identifies product categories that contribute most strongly to
marketplace sales.

## 5. Operational KPIs

### Average Delivery Days

**Definition:**\
Average number of days required to deliver completed orders.

**Business Purpose:**\
Measures fulfillment performance and helps identify potential delivery
inefficiencies.

### Cancellation Rate

**Definition:**\
Percentage of orders that were cancelled.

**Calculation:**\
Cancelled Orders / Total Orders × 100

**Business Purpose:**\
Provides an indication of order loss and potential
operational/customer-experience issues.

## 6. Customer Experience KPI

### Average Review Score

**Definition:**\
Average customer review score on a 1--5 scale.

**Business Purpose:**\
Provides an overall indicator of customer satisfaction.

## 7. Payment Analysis

Payment method usage was analyzed as a behavioral metric rather than a
direct performance KPI.

It helps identify:

-   Most frequently used payment methods
-   Installment behavior
-   Customer payment preferences

The analysis does not assume that a particular payment method should be
promoted without additional evidence such as conversion rate,
transaction cost, customer demographics or profitability.

## Conclusion

The KPI framework focuses on metrics that directly support the project's
business questions and dashboard requirements. The selected KPIs provide
a balanced view of sales, customers, products, operations and customer
experience.
