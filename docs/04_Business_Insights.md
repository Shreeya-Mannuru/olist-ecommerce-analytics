# 4. Business Insights

## 1. Sales Performance

The analysis generated approximately R\$13.22 million in revenue from
delivered orders, across approximately 99K orders.

The average order value was approximately R\$137.04, providing an
indication of the typical value generated by a completed transaction.

The monthly trend analysis shows that marketplace activity changed
considerably over the observed period. Monthly revenue and order volume
should therefore be evaluated alongside the time period rather than
using the overall totals alone.

## 2. Customer Base

The marketplace contains approximately 96K unique customers in the
analyzed dataset.

The difference between total orders and unique customers indicates that
some customers placed multiple orders, while others placed only one
order.

The repeat-customer analysis provides a more meaningful view of customer
retention than simply counting total orders.

**Dashboard-specific observation:**\
The final Power BI dashboard should be used to identify the exact
repeat-customer rate and determine whether repeat purchasing represents
a significant opportunity.

## 3. Product Performance

Revenue is not distributed equally across all product categories.

The Top 10 Product Categories analysis identifies the categories
contributing the greatest share of revenue.

These categories should receive greater attention when evaluating:

-   Inventory availability
-   Seller performance
-   Delivery reliability
-   Customer satisfaction
-   Future commercial opportunities

The analysis should not assume that high revenue automatically means
high profitability because product-level cost and margin information is
not available in the dataset.

## 4. Delivery Performance

The average delivery time for delivered orders was approximately 12.09
days.

Delivery performance is an important operational factor because delays
can negatively influence customer satisfaction.

Delivery performance should therefore be evaluated alongside review
scores rather than as an isolated KPI.

## 5. Customer Satisfaction

The average review score was approximately 4.09 out of 5.

Overall, this indicates that customer feedback was generally positive.

However, an overall average can hide poor experiences among a smaller
group of customers. The review-score distribution should therefore also
be monitored to identify the proportion of low-rated orders.

## 6. Order Status

The order-status analysis provides context for the overall order volume.

Most orders in the dataset are completed/delivered, while a smaller
proportion fall into statuses such as cancelled, shipped, processing or
unavailable.

This distinction is important because total orders should not
automatically be interpreted as completed sales.

For revenue analysis, delivered orders were therefore used as the
primary completed-sales population.

## 7. Payment Behavior

The payment-method analysis shows how customers currently transact on
the marketplace.

Payment method usage should be interpreted as customer behavior rather
than as evidence that one particular payment method should be promoted.

A stronger commercial decision would require additional information such
as:

-   Payment processing costs
-   Conversion rates
-   Customer segment preferences
-   Transaction failure rates
-   Installment economics

## 8. Seller Geography

Seller-state analysis shows that marketplace activity is geographically
concentrated rather than evenly distributed across all states.

High-revenue seller locations can be investigated further to understand:

-   Seller concentration
-   Logistics performance
-   Regional customer demand
-   Delivery differences
-   Category specialization

## 9. Overall Business Insight

The analysis shows that the marketplace has substantial transaction
volume and revenue, with generally positive customer feedback.

The strongest opportunities are not simply to increase order volume, but
to improve the relationship between:

**Sales → Delivery → Customer Satisfaction → Repeat Purchasing**

This provides a more useful business perspective than examining
individual KPIs independently.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides a multidimensional view of marketplace performance
across sales, customers, products, operations and customer experience.

The findings indicate that revenue and order growth should be evaluated
together with delivery performance, customer satisfaction and repeat
purchasing behavior to support sustainable marketplace performance.
